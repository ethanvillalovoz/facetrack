# FaceTrack design direction

FaceTrack should feel like an inspection instrument, not a generic analytics dashboard. The image and the decision ledger are the product: selecting a face in either place selects the same record, so the reviewer can trace each attendance decision without hunting across the screen.

## Principles

- Keep the session frame and match ledger visible together on wide screens.
- Make detection, identity, distance, and decision traceable as one synchronized record.
- Treat privacy as an operating constraint, not a marketing badge.
- Use the Georgia Tech red sparingly for selection and commands; reserve green and amber for outcomes.
- Prefer real recognition outputs and privacy-safe staged frames over decorative charts.
- Keep roster and attendance views utilitarian and consistent with the session workspace.

## Avoid

- Generic KPI cards, gradients, oversized metrics, or facial-recognition futurism.
- Claims that imply cloud processing, surveillance, or accuracy that the repository does not demonstrate.
- Synthetic demo states that cannot be reproduced from the bundled demo provider.
